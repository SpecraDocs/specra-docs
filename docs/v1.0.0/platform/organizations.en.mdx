# Organizations

Organizations let teams collaborate on documentation projects with role-based access control. Organizations are available on **Pro** and **Enterprise** plans.

## Creating an Organization

1. Go to your [dashboard](https://specra-docs.com/dashboard)
2. Click **Organizations** in the sidebar
3. Click **Create Organization**
4. Enter a name and optional description

The person who creates the organization automatically becomes the **Owner**.

## Roles

Each organization member has one of three roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access. Manage billing, members, roles, and all projects. Delete the organization. One owner per org. |
| **Admin** | Manage members and projects. Cannot change billing or delete the organization. |
| **Member** | View and deploy to assigned projects. Cannot manage members or billing. |

<Callout type="info">
  Ownership can be transferred to another member from **Organization Settings > General**.
</Callout>

## Inviting Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Organization">
    Go to **Organizations > Your Org > Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an Invitation">
    Click **Invite Member**, enter their email address, and choose a role (Admin or Member).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acceptance">
    The invitee receives an email with a link to accept the invitation. They must have a Specra account (or create one) to join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pending invitations count toward your seat usage. You can cancel pending invitations from the Members page.

## Seat Management

Your plan determines how many seats are included and how much extra seats cost:

| Plan | Base Seats | Extra Seat Cost |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Pro | 10 | $8/seat/month |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Included |

### Buying Extra Seats

If you need more seats than your plan includes:

1. Go to **Organization Settings > Billing**
2. Click **Add Seats**
3. Choose how many additional seats you need
4. Extra seats are billed monthly on top of your plan price

<Callout type="tip">
  Extra seat charges are prorated. If you add a seat mid-cycle, you only pay for the remaining days in that billing period.
</Callout>

### Removing Members

Admins and Owners can remove members from **Organization > Members**. Removing a member frees up a seat immediately.

## Organization Projects

Projects can belong to an organization rather than an individual user. All organization members with appropriate permissions can access organization projects.

To move a personal project into an organization:

1. Go to **Project Settings > General**
2. Under **Transfer Project**, select the target organization
3. Confirm the transfer

<Callout type="warning">
  Transferring a project to an organization is irreversible from the dashboard. Contact support if you need to reverse it.
</Callout>
