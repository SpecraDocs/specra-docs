# Analytics

Specra Platform includes built-in, privacy-friendly analytics for your documentation sites. No third-party scripts, no cookie banners — just the data you need to understand how your docs are being used.

<Callout type="info">
  Analytics requires a **Starter** plan or above. Free plan projects do not have access to analytics.
</Callout>

## What We Track

### Page Views and Visitors

- **Total page views** — how many times your pages were loaded
- **Unique visitors** — distinct sessions visiting your site
- **Bounce rate** — percentage of visitors who viewed only one page
- **Average session duration** — how long visitors spend on your docs
- **Scroll depth** — how far down the page visitors scroll
- **Top pages** — your most visited documentation pages

### Geography

- **Country** — which countries your visitors come from
- **Region** — state or province-level data
- **City** — city-level location data

### Devices

- **Browser** — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.
- **Operating system** — Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android
- **Device type** — Desktop, mobile, or tablet

### Referrers

See where your traffic comes from — search engines, social media, direct links, or other websites.

### Sessions

Each visitor session is tracked with a unique session ID. Sessions are used to calculate bounce rate, session duration, and unique visitor counts.

## Time Periods

You can view analytics for four time windows:

| Period | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **24 hours** | Last day of activity |
| **7 days** | Last week |
| **30 days** | Last month |
| **90 days** | Last quarter |

## Realtime Visitors

The dashboard shows the number of visitors currently active on your site (active within the last 5 minutes).

## Accessing Analytics

Navigate to your project in the dashboard and click **Analytics** in the sidebar. You'll see an overview with:

- Total views and unique visitors
- Bounce rate and average duration
- Top pages chart
- Geographic breakdown map
- Device and browser charts
- Referrer list

## Privacy

Specra Analytics is designed with privacy in mind:

- No cookies are used for tracking
- No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected
- Data is stored securely and never shared with third parties
- Compliant with GDPR and CCPA requirements
- No cookie consent banner required

<Callout type="tip">
  Because Specra Analytics doesn't use cookies, you don't need to show a cookie consent banner on your documentation site.
</Callout>
