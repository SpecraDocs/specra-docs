## Plan Details

### Free

Perfect for personal projects and getting started. You get one project with a custom domain, 7 days of version history, and full access to all Specra SDK components.

### Starter — $19/month

For small teams and growing projects. Includes analytics, contact forms, and up to 4 projects. Add extra team seats for $5/seat/month.

### Pro — $49/month

For professional teams that need collaboration features. Includes GitHub integration, live chat, organizations with role-based access, and unlimited version history. Add extra seats for $8/seat/month.

### Enterprise — Custom Pricing

For large organizations with advanced requirements. Includes everything in Pro plus SSO/SAML, audit logs, a dedicated SLA, and priority support. [Contact us](mailto:hello@specra-docs.com) for a custom quote.

## Upgrading and Downgrading

You can change your plan at any time from **Account Settings > Billing**. When upgrading, you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle. When downgrading, the new rate applies at the start of your next billing cycle.

<Callout type="warning">
  Downgrading may disable features that aren't available on your new plan. Make sure to review what you'll lose before confirming.
</Callout>

## FAQ

**Can I try Pro features before committing?**
Contact us for a 14-day trial of any paid plan.

**What happens if I exceed my project limit?**
You won't be able to create new projects until you upgrade or delete existing ones. Existing projects continue to work normally.

**Do you offer discounts for open-source projects?**
Yes! Open-source projects can apply for a free Pro plan. [Contact us](mailto:hello@specra-docs.com) with a link to your repository.
