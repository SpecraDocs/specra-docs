# Billing

Manage your subscription, payment methods, and invoices from your account settings.

## Subscription Management

### Viewing Your Plan

Go to **Account Settings > Billing** to see your current plan, billing cycle, next payment date, and usage.

### Changing Plans

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time:

- **Upgrading** takes effect immediately. You're charged a prorated amount for the rest of your current billing cycle.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. You keep your current features until then.

### Cancelling

To cancel your subscription, go to **Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan**. Your account reverts to the Free plan at the end of your current billing cycle. Your projects remain accessible, but features exclusive to paid plans will be disabled.

<Callout type="warning">
  If you have more projects than the Free plan allows (1 project), you'll need to delete extra projects or they will become read-only.
</Callout>

## Payment Providers

Specra supports four payment providers to serve customers worldwide:

### Stripe (Global)

The default payment provider. Accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and other major credit and debit cards. Also supports Apple Pay and Google Pay where available.

### M-Pesa (Kenya)

Pay directly from your M-Pesa mobile money account. Available for customers in Kenya. Payments are processed in KES.

### Pesapal (Africa)

Supports mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN), bank transfers, and cards across multiple African countries.

### NOWPayments (Cryptocurrency)

Pay with Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT, and 100+ other cryptocurrencies. Payments are converted at the current exchange rate.

<Callout type="info">
  You can switch payment providers at any time from your billing settings. Your next payment will use the new provider.
</Callout>

## Invoices

Invoices are generated automatically for every payment. You can view and download invoices from **Account Settings > Billing > Invoices**.

Each invoice includes:

- Invoice number and date
- Plan name and billing period
- Line items (base plan, extra seats, etc.)
- Tax amount (if applicable)
- Total amount and payment method

Invoices are available as PDF downloads.

## Coupons

If you have a coupon code, apply it during checkout or from **Account Settings > Billing > Coupon**. Coupons can provide:

- **Percentage discounts** (e.g., 20% off for 3 months)
- **Fixed amount discounts** (e.g., $10 off your next payment)
- **Extended trials** (e.g., 30-day free trial of Pro)

<Callout type="tip">
  Coupons are applied once and cannot be combined. If you already have an active coupon, the new one replaces it.
</Callout>

## Billing FAQ

**When am I charged?**
You're charged at the beginning of each billing cycle (monthly or annually).

**Can I get a refund?**
We offer refunds within 7 days of a charge. Contact support at [hello@specra-docs.com](mailto:hello@specra-docs.com).

**What happens if a payment fails?**
We retry failed payments up to 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, your account is downgraded to the Free plan.

**Do you charge tax?**
Tax is applied based on your billing address and local regulations.
